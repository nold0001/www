This English version is a translation provided for reference; if there is any difference, the Korean version prevails.

Effective date: November 1, 2026

2. Receiving promotional information

News emails are sent only to those who separately agreed to [Optional] consent to receive promotional information when applying for the trial. Not agreeing does not affect your use of the trial. Each email has "(Ad)" before the subject and the sender and an unsubscribe link at the end of the body; clicking the link stops emails immediately. Emails are not sent between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m.

3. Provision to third parties

The Company does not provide personal information to third parties except with the data subject's consent or where the law specifically provides otherwise.

5. Destruction procedure and method

Personal information whose retention period has ended or whose purpose has been fulfilled is destroyed without delay. Electronic files are erased in a way that cannot be recovered, and paper documents are shredded or incinerated.

6. Rights of data subjects and how to exercise them

Data subjects may at any time request access to, correction or deletion of, suspension of processing of their personal information, or withdraw consent. Send your request to contact@bronto.co.kr, and after verifying your identity we will act within 10 days and inform you of the result. Requests can also be made through a representative. If you request deletion during a trial, the trial ends as well.

7. Security measures

  • Administrator access is verified in two steps, a password and a one-time code (OTP), and access rights are limited to those in charge.
  • Transmission is encrypted with HTTPS, and passwords and authentication tokens are stored as hashes, not in plain text.
  • Records of administrators changing or deleting personal information are kept in a tamper-proof audit log.
  • Measures are in place to block automated applications, and blocked emails, domains and PCs cannot apply again.

8. Cookies and other automatic collection

The website (www.bronto.co.kr) and the trial application screen do not use cookies or analytics tools that track visitors. However, the hosting and CDN services in section 4 may keep access logs for delivery.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, notice is posted on this page from 7 days before it takes effect. Changes important to data subjects' rights are announced 30 days in advance.
