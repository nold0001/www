HOW IT WORKS

From field equipment to operators, all connected

tems receives and controls device signals, and tOps manages the status and settings of tems servers remotely. tVault handles installing, updating and licensing all of these programs.

Field

NEW · tVault

New versions rolled out safely, and only to customer servers that were approved

Release and deployment are separate steps. Rollouts widen from internal to pilot to customers, and the customer's contact approves by choosing devices and an installation time from a link sent by email. When it finishes, a result email with the update time of each device is sent.
